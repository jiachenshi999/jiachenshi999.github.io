01 / Research

Making road quality measurable.

My work connects pavement materials, construction processes, and sensing technologies. A central question is how to detect segregation and other quality variations early enough to improve decisions on site.

01

Construction quality sensing

Using infrared and visual data to identify temperature and material segregation during asphalt paving.

02

Image-based evaluation

Quantifying aggregate structure and pavement uniformity with image processing and deep learning.

03

Pavement performance

Studying materials, durability, and infrastructure condition to support better maintenance decisions.
